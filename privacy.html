Privacy Policy for Acoustic Twin

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Acoustic Twin ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application "Acoustic Twin // NEXUS PROTOCOL".

2. Information We Collect

Audio Data: The app requires access to your device's microphone to perform acoustic analysis.

  • Passive Scan: Listens to ambient sounds to detect mechanical anomalies.
  • Sonar Scan: Emits a high-frequency sound (19kHz) and analyzes the echo.
Important: All audio processing happens locally on your device. We do NOT record, store, or transmit any audio data to external servers.

3. Permissions Required

  • Microphone: Required for acoustic analysis and sonar functionality.
  • Vibrate: Used for haptic feedback during scanning.

4. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach from our side. All diagnostic reports are generated locally and are not stored permanently unless you choose to save them.

5. GDPR Compliance (For EU Users)

We do not collect Personal Identifiable Information (PII). Therefore, no consent for data processing is required under GDPR. You have full control over the app's access to your microphone via your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
